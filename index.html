Fair questions

Asked by every business owner.

How long does a website take?+

A site like this preview typically takes 2–4 weeks from first call to launch — mostly depending on how quickly photos and content come together.

What do you need from us?+

Your logo (or we'll design one), photos of the business, and an hour of your time to tell us what makes it good. We handle everything else.

How much does it cost?+

Every business is different, so we quote after a short conversation — fixed price, in writing, before any work starts. No hourly billing, no surprises.

Will it work on phones?+

Most of your customers will visit on a phone, so every page is designed mobile-first and checked on real screen sizes before launch.

Can we update it ourselves later?+

Yes. We build on systems you can edit, and we record a short walkthrough showing exactly how — no developer required for everyday changes.
