Privacy policy.

Sample  Preview template — finalised with the business before launch. Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect, and why

When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), and your message. That's it. We use it for one purpose: to reply to your enquiry.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your details with anyone.
  • We don't add you to marketing lists — the form has no hidden newsletter signup.
  • We don't use analytics cookies or tracking pixels on this site, so there's nothing to opt out of.

Third parties

None. This site loads no third-party resources at all — fonts, images, styles, and scripts are all served from the site's own hosting. The only outside service involved is the web host delivering the pages (which keeps standard server logs). Nobody receives your form details except us.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months so we can follow up properly, then deleted. Ask us to delete yours sooner at any time — email us and it's done within 7 days.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Contact details for these requests will be published here on the live site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears here with a new date. Significant changes are announced on the site.
